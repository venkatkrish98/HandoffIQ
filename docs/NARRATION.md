# Demo narration (target 3:30, hard limit < 5:00)

Spoken text, one block per scene. Do not record until the live end-to-end run (Jira + Confluence) passes.

## 1. The problem (0:00)
When work moves between teams, the context is scattered. Requirements live in Confluence. Tasks live in Jira. Decisions hide in meeting notes. An ordinary AI summary smooths over the conflicts and gaps. HandoffIQ is different. It gives you a handover you can verify, and nothing changes in your workspace until you approve it.

## 2. Create a handover (0:25)
I'm inside Jira, running HandoffIQ, a Forge app. I name the handover, set the teams and the expected delivery date. Then I pick my sources: a Jira project and Confluence pages. HandoffIQ reads only what my own Atlassian permissions allow.

## 3. Evidence-linked analysis (0:55)
HandoffIQ retrieved the real issues and pages, and extracted requirements, decisions, dependencies and risks. Every item quotes the exact sentence it came from and links back to the source. Each one is labelled: verified fact, inference, missing information, or assumption.

## 4. Conflicts and gaps (1:35)
Here is what a summary would hide. Two sources name different owners. Two sources give different go-live dates, and neither matches the date on the handover. Some Jira issues have no assignee. Each problem shows both sides, side by side.

## 5. Readiness and questions (2:10)
The readiness score is fully explained. Seven weighted dimensions, each with its reason. It measures how complete the information is, not whether the project will succeed. The open problems become clarification questions. I can edit them, assign an owner, record a response, and re-run the analysis without losing my work.

## 6. Human approval (2:40)
Now the part that matters. HandoffIQ proposes Jira tasks and a Confluence handover page. I review exactly what will be created, choose the project and the space, and approve. Forge creates them as me, with my permissions.

## 7. Real results and audit (3:10)
Here are the real Jira issues, with real keys, and the real Confluence page. Every action is in the audit trail: who approved it, when, and the result.

## 8. Rovo agent and close (3:30)
The HandoffIQ Rovo agent lets teams ask which handovers are not ready and why. It can propose changes, but it can never approve them. HandoffIQ: never lose context between teams.
